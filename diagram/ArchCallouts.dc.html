The decisions worth interrogating
1
Refuse before you guess
The hierarchy is a per-family rulebook, and five fit checks decide whether it actually fits this document. Unknown units in the interpretation clause, numbering the grammar does not cover, text that attaches to no node, depth out of range, indentation disagreeing with numbering. Any alarm quarantines the document with evidence for a person. A confidently wrong tree corrupts every citation built on it, so the one unrecoverable failure is the one we refuse to risk.
2
References are nodes with a lifecycle
A citation can fail, hesitate, or need sign-off, so it graduates from edge to node. Detection and resolution are separate, separately scored steps. Resolution uses the rules the contract itself stipulates, and the document breaks its own rule three times, "Clause 1.x" written inside Schedules. Those resolve to the stipulated target, carry both candidates, and go to review. Unresolved never disappears, it waits for its target document to arrive, then flips.
3
Errors have different prices
A stray capital mints a phantom term, a missing capital hides an obligation from search, and the second usually costs more. So the eval reports a confusion matrix with cost weights a domain expert sets, not bare accuracy. Typo-dense sections get their own routing, because there capitalisation stops being evidence in both directions. And audits sample confident matches too, stratified by term length, part and position, since checking only the self-declared hard cases lets systematic easy-case errors run silently.
4
Derive everything, then diff it
The assignment ships a page map, an outline and a definitions schedule. None is imported. The pipeline derives all three independently and diffs, and every disagreement is triaged, parser wrong, provided artifact wrong, or both differ from the page. Sampled disagreements go to a person whose verdicts tune the derivers, measured on documents the tuning never saw. The notes say 46 parts, their own table lists 48, and the derived map reports what the document supports.
5
A trust gradient, not a blend
Structure is deterministic and replayable. Vocabulary is rule-derived with its own internal dependency graph, terms defined using other terms. Concepts are model-derived, flagged, and never citable, a concept collides with a declared term and the term keeps the job. Confidence is calibrated, deterministic resolvers carry measured precision, models score before they commit to an answer, and no number ships that has never met ground truth.
6
Built for the ten-thousandth document
Deterministic ids make reingestion an upsert, batch tags make any load reversible. Amendments are new version roots with SUPERSEDES, one clause's history is a lineage-key query, copy on write when storage bites. Salience is breadth times log-damped frequency, so being needed from many places beats being repeated. What breaks first is cross-document resolution, and the answer is scoped search within a collection plus review, not a bigger model.
