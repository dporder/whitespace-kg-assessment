01
Documents arrive
Continuously, in mixed shapes and quality. Native text PDFs like this one. Scans and photographs with no text layer. Amendments to documents already in the graph.
NEWOne 475 page framework agreement, 46 bound parts, definitions, forms and schedules.
02
Profile it, and decide whether we understand it
Pick the rulebook for this document family, the unit names, numbering grammar and citation habits. Then five checks that the rulebook truly fits, the interpretation clause names units we know, the numbering matches the grammar, almost no text is left homeless, depth is in range, and indentation agrees with numbering. Each page is also routed up an extraction ladder, read the text layer where one exists, layout OCR for scans, vision models only where cheaper rungs disagree.
exampleA document opening "In this Agreement, Article means..." fails check one. Wrong rulebook, stop.
03
Read the pages into words with positions
Every word keeps its box on the page. Headers and footers are stripped by position and repetition, but each part's printed page number is kept, it is what a lawyer quotes. Part boundaries are found where the footer signature changes. Columns come from box geometry rather than line order, and tables keep their cells, each with its own box.
exampleThe definitions schedule is two columns and terms wrap mid cell. Reading by line order shreds it, reading by geometry does not.
04
Assemble the tree the numbering describes
Clause 3.1.2 nests under 3.1 under 3. A node holds text or holds children, never both, and a clause's lead-in sentence becomes its own child so every character has exactly one home. The tree is then checked against the printed page, a child's box must sit inside its parent's, and 3.1.2 must sit further right than 3.1. Oddities are recorded exactly as found, never repaired.
exampleThe brief says 35 clauses, a plain pattern finds 34, so the odd heading is hunted and its difference logged. A stray typo, rFramework, stays verbatim.
05
Load into the graph, safely rerunnable
Every node's identity is derived from its position, document, version, part, path, so loading twice updates rather than duplicates. Every load is tagged and reversible as a batch. A salience score is computed for ranking, breadth of citation times log damped frequency, so being needed from many places beats being repeated.
versioningAn amendment becomes a new version. One clause's history is a single indexed lookup across versions.

References, found then resolved
A citation grammar finds the pointing words, and an orphan scan catches any citation word the grammar missed. Resolution follows the rules the contract itself stipulates, in its interpretation clause, about what "Clause" and "Paragraph" mean where.
hard casesWhat the rules cannot settle goes to a small model shown the top five candidates, allowed to answer none. Still unsure goes to human review. Unresolved is kept, never dropped.
Vocabulary, declared and discovered
The definitions schedule gives about 300 capitalised terms. A discovery rule finds definition sites independently, and the two lists are diffed. Matching is case sensitive, longest match wins, abbreviations count as the term.
hard casesSentence starts, headings and typo dense text each route to their own narrow model check, then to review.
Concepts, for finding
A model tags each section with the ideas it covers, so an agent can narrow to a neighbourhood no keyword names. Tags are flagged as model derived and are never citable, and a tag that collides with a defined term is not created, the term keeps the job.
Embeddings, three altitudes
Leaves are embedded as raw text. Containers get a within budget concatenation and a short summary vector for altitude. A hit on generated text must resolve down to real leaf text before anything is cited from it.
Quarantine, a person decides
The document stops with the failing check and examples attached. A wrong tree would corrupt every citation built on it, so the pipeline refuses rather than guesses. With the right rulebook chosen, it re-enters at step 02.
06
Evaluate, before anyone relies on it
Structural checks first, every character attached, numbering coherent, geometry agreeing with the tree. Then the derive and diff pass, the page map, outline and definitions list we derived against the ones supplied, every disagreement triaged, we were wrong, the artifact was wrong, or both differ from the page. Then a hand labelled golden set scoring finding and resolving separately, where declining to resolve is also scored, and a resolved reference that should have been unresolvable fails the whole load. Confidence numbers are calibrated against these labels, and random confident cases are audited too, stratified by term length, part and position, so easy case errors cannot hide.
gatesAny failure blocks release. The load exists, the release does not.
Review queue
Each item shows the sentence, the page image with the box drawn, and the candidates. Approve, pick, or reject. Minutes of judgement, not hours of reading.

[ HIGHLIGHT ]
References are nodes, not edges
This domain runs on cross references, about 1,500 in this one document, so they get first class treatment. A citation can fail, hesitate or need sign off, so it has a lifecycle and a status. Unresolved waits visibly for its target document to arrive, then flips.
[ HIGHLIGHT ]
Derive everything, then diff it
Nothing supplied is imported, the page map, outline and definitions are re-derived and compared. Their notes say 46 parts, their own table lists 48. The derived map reports what the document supports.
[ HIGHLIGHT ]
Errors have different prices
A stray capital mints a phantom term, a missing one hides an obligation, and the second costs more. The eval reports a cost weighted confusion matrix with expert set weights, not bare accuracy.
