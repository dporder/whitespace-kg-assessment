Who owns new intellectual property created under a contract, and what is that ownership subject to?
Assistant
Any new intellectual property created under a contract is owned by the Central Buying Office1 — the central purchasing authority the agreement defines at the front.2
That ownership is not unconditional. It is expressly subject to the supply obligations in Clause 3.1: the Provider must supply Outputs that meet the requirement and comply with the law,3 and must supply them with a warranty of at least 90 days from handover against obvious defects.4
Two things in that same sentence I could not settle. It also points at a “Schedule 2” without saying which one, and this document set contains more than one; that is sitting in the review queue rather than in this answer. It cites the Bribery Act 2010, which is a piece of legislation rather than part of this agreement.
